## Diagnosis
The report builder preferred walking-route geometry for overview map markers whenever walking metrics existed, while the map legend and the table's main route describe road travel. A pedestrian path can terminate at a different access point and make hotel markers appear displaced from their expected road route. Keep road route geometry for the overview, keep walking metrics separate, and make any displaced label's exact-coordinate anchor visible.
